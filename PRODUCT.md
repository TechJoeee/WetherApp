# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Casual users checking today's weather quickly, primarily from a phone or laptop.

## Product Purpose

WetherApp makes it easy to search for a place and understand its current conditions and near-term forecast at a glance. Success means a user can answer “what is the weather like there?” within seconds.

## Positioning

This is a fun learning project focused on making weather data feel clear and alive through a polished interface, smooth transitions, and condition-aware visuals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- City search and geocoding
- Current conditions and a five-day forecast
- Celsius/Fahrenheit switching
- Responsive mobile and desktop layout
- Vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and Tailwind CSS
- Open-Meteo is the initial weather data source
- No accounts or server-side persistence

## Product Principles

- Make the current answer obvious before the detail.
- Let motion explain a state change.
- Keep controls familiar and lightweight.
- Treat mobile as a first-class surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, and reduced-motion preferences.
